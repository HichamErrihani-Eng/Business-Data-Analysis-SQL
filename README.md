## 🎯 Project Overview

This repository is a **professional portfolio** of end-to-end data analysis projects built around **real business scenarios**. Each project demonstrates the ability to transform raw data into **actionable insights** that drive strategic decision-making.

From customer behavior analysis to financial reporting, every project follows a complete, industry-standard workflow:

1.  **Data Exploration** – Understanding the dataset, its structure, and its limitations.
2.  **Data Cleaning** – Handling missing values, duplicates, and inconsistencies.
3.  **Advanced Analysis** – Writing complex SQL queries (joins, subqueries, window functions, CTEs) to answer critical business questions.
4.  **Visualization** – Presenting findings through Tableau dashboards and Python notebooks.
5.  **Business Recommendations** – Translating data into clear, strategic recommendations.

## 📂 Featured Projects

### 1. 📱 Customer Behavior Analysis – Instagram Clone (MySQL)
**Business Problem:** A social media platform wants to increase user engagement and retention.
**Analysis:**
- Identified the most rewarding system for loyal users.
- Determined the best weekdays for launching registration campaigns.
- Segmented inactive users for re-engagement campaigns.
**Outcome:** Provided data-driven recommendations to boost daily active users.

### 2. 🎬 Sales & Marketing Optimization – Film Rental Store (MySQL)
**Business Problem:** A DVD rental store needs to understand customer preferences to optimize inventory.
**Analysis:**
- Analyzed rental patterns by genre, duration, and customer demographics.
- Measured the effectiveness of past marketing campaigns.
- Identified high-value customers and their rental behaviors.
**Outcome:** Delivered insights to reduce stockouts and improve marketing ROI.

### 3. 👥 HR Analytics – Employee Demographics (MySQL & Tableau)
**Business Problem:** Management needs a clear breakdown of workforce diversity and compensation.
**Analysis:**
- Calculated the male-to-female employee ratio per department (1990–present).
- Compared average salaries by gender and department.
- Tracked the number of male vs. female managers over time.
**Outcome:** Built an interactive Tableau dashboard for HR decision-making.

### 4. 🌍 Financial Reporting – International Debt Statistics (PostgreSQL)
**Business Problem:** An economic research firm needs to analyze global debt patterns.
**Analysis:**
- Calculated average debt across countries.
- Identified the maximum debt indicators and the countries associated with them.
- Ranked countries by total debt and debt type.
**Outcome:** Delivered a comprehensive report on global debt trends.

### 5. 🚗 Road Safety Analysis – UK Accidents 2015 (MySQL & Python)
**Business Problem:** A government agency wants to reduce road accidents.
**Analysis:**
- Calculated median severity of accidents by vehicle type.
- Analyzed total accidents and average severity per motorcycle category.
**Outcome:** Provided insights for targeted safety campaigns.

### 6. 🎵 Retail Analytics – Digital Music Store (PostgreSQL)
**Business Problem:** An online music store wants to increase revenue.
**Analysis:**
- Identified countries with the most invoices.
- Found the city with the best customers.
- Determined the top-spending customer and their purchase history.
**Outcome:** Recommended targeted marketing strategies for high-value regions.

### 7. 🍽️ Operational Analytics – Restaurant & Library (SQLite)
**Business Problem:** A restaurant and a library need to manage customer data and reservations efficiently.
**Analysis:**
- Managed customer information, reservations, and orders for a 20th-anniversary event.
- Tracked book loans, due dates, and patron activity.
**Outcome:** Streamlined operations and improved customer experience.
